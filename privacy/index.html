English

MiseSeikyu is a local iOS app for creating estimates, invoices, receipts, customer records, and PDF files.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It does not use analytics, ads, tracking SDKs, or account systems.

Local Storage

Business details, customers, documents, line items, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, the local app data may be deleted by iOS.

User-Initiated Sharing

When you export or share a PDF, you choose the destination through the iOS share sheet. The app does not control the privacy practices of other apps or services you select.

Network

The app does not automatically make network requests. Privacy and support pages open only when you tap their links.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact jay212315@gmail.com.
