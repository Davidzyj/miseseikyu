Mise Invoice / 小店請求書

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

English

Mise Invoice is a local-first iOS app for creating invoices, receipts, estimates, delivery notes, and simple monthly summaries.

Data Collection

The app does not collect personal data, does not use tracking, does not show ads, and does not use analytics SDKs.

Local Storage

Shop profiles, clients, document records, line items, and generated files are stored on your device. The app does not send this data to a server.

Network Use

The app does not automatically initiate network requests. Privacy Policy and Support links open only when you tap them.

Third Parties

The app does not share data with third parties. When you choose to share a PDF through iOS share features, your selected destination is controlled by you and by the chosen service.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact jay212315@gmail.com.

中文

小店請求書是一款本地优先的 iOS 应用，用于创建请款单、收据、报价单、交货单和简单月度汇总。

数据收集

本应用不收集个人数据，不进行跟踪，不展示广告，也不使用分析 SDK。

本地存储

店铺资料、客户、文档记录、明细和生成的文件保存在你的设备上。应用不会把这些数据发送到服务器。

网络使用

应用不会主动发起网络请求。隐私政策和支持页面仅在你点击链接时打开。

第三方

应用不会向第三方共享数据。当你通过 iOS 分享功能发送 PDF 时，接收方由你选择，并受所选服务控制。

联系

如有隐私相关问题，请联系 jay212315@gmail.com。
